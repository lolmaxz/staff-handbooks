# Host Dashboard

The **Host Dashboard** is the main place hosts and trials check their week — quota, submitted slots, and assigned events.

**How to get here:** Click **Host** in the main navigation (top bar on desktop, bottom bar on phone). The EDEN logo also returns you to Host.

## Empty Week — Add Availability

If you have not submitted for the selected week, the dashboard shows an empty state. Click **Add Availabilities** on the availability card to open the form.

/>

## Submitted for the Week

After you submit, the dashboard shows your **quota** and saved availability slots.

<ResponsiveScreenshot
  src="scheduler/10-host-dashboard-filled"
  alt="Host dashboard showing quota and submitted availability slots"
/>

:::tip Two kinds of quota
**Week quota** (on this form or on the Host dashboard for one week) is how many events you want **that week**. **Recurring weekly quota** (top of the **Recurring** page) is the default cap for weeks your rules auto-fill. Per-week edits win for that week — see [Recurring Availability](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-host-recurring#weekly-quota).
:::

## Upcoming Events

Once managers assign you on the builder, **Upcoming Events** lists what you are scheduled to host.

## Event Library

Scroll down the Host page for **Event Library** — saved event types and collections you reuse on the availability form. Open an event type to upload a SFW image or design one in [Banner Studio](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-banner-studio).

## Next Steps

- Edit availability: click **Add Availability**, **Add Availabilities**, or **Edit Availabilities** on the week card → [Host Availability](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-host-availability)
- Set repeating weeks: click **Recurring** in the navigation, or **Recurring availability** on this page → [Recurring Availability](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-host-recurring)
- Personal calendar: click the **Calendar** button at the top of this page (not the main nav **Calendar** link) → [Host Calendar](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-host-calendar)
- Event type banners: **Event Library** on this page → [Banner Studio](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-banner-studio)
