# Schedule History

:::caution Legacy — posterity only
This handbook page exists because the portal route and screenshot were already documented — **not** because Schedule History is part of normal staff workflow. The feature **may be removed** from the scheduler later. **Do not expect this page to be updated** as the product changes.
:::

**Schedule History** (`/schedules`) lists **published** past weeks in read-only form. Some managers use it to look up who hosted what after Push; for day-to-day work, prefer the [Manager Dashboard](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-manager-dashboard), [Schedule Builder](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-schedule-builder), [Audit Log](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-audit-log), and [Shared Calendar](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-shared-calendar).

**How to get here:** Open shared **Calendar** (main nav **Calendar**), then click the **Schedule History** icon in the top-left corner of the page.

:::info Read-only
History weeks cannot be edited here. New weeks are built via **Open Schedule Builder for this week** on the [Manager Dashboard](/docs/general-handbook/eden-scheduler/eden-scheduler-manager-dashboard).
:::
