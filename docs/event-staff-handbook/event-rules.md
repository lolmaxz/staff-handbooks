## Attendance Qualification

To attend an event, a member must:

- Be a **Verified** member of the server.
- Be at least **New User** rank in VRChat (blue shield icon / blue profile border). Hephia refuses signups from lower ranks.
- Be able to communicate. Desktop, mobile, or being mute is fine on its own, but a member **cannot** be **both** on Desktop **and** mute, **unless** they can clearly communicate with the text box or a good TTS (text-to-speech). This must be arranged **ahead of time in a ticket**, so staff can check their eligibility.

## The Clear "NO" Rule

The **Clear "NO"** rule is signified by crossing your arms in an "X" shape. It is a definitive "no": whatever the person is doing with or to the one making the sign must stop immediately. This applies to everyone, including staff. As Hosts or Security, you are responsible for watching out for this sign and ensuring others respect it.

:::note Consent is Priority #1
As always, consent is our number one priority. Desktop users can use the 🚫 emote, or simply say it out loud.
:::

## The Eden Consent Badges

Eden Consent Badges are visual indicators that members can add to their avatars. They help communicate consent preferences. Hosts and Security should recognize what each badge color means:

- **🔴 Red**: No consent; do not touch; do not ask.
- **🟡 Yellow**: Consent to touch, but you must ask first.
- **🟢 Green**: Consent to touch is given; no need to ask.
- **🔵 Blue**: Same as green, but actively looking to be lewd.

## Not Allowed (Zero Tolerance)

Depending on the severity, sanctions go from a **kick from the ongoing event** up to a **permanent event ban**.

:::info Late joiners & knowing the rules
Staff are **not** required to re-announce event rules to members who join after the initial start-of-event announcement. Members are expected to know the rules in <ChannelBadge variant="post" label="💠｜rules" link="https://discord.com/channels/734595073920204940/737074569319546921" /> (and any event-specific rules on the Discord event post) before joining. Hosts must put any **event-specific** rules in the public event announcement.
:::

## How to Report Something

Open <ChannelBadge label="🎫｜open-a-ticket" link="https://discord.com/channels/734595073920204940/1106413750975746070" /> and choose <strong>Event‑Talk</strong>. Only the highest staff members, the Event Manager, and their assistants can see the ticket.

## Host Discretion

Hosts decide what is appropriate for **their** event — including avatar / theme expectations — and may ask members to switch avis or leave if they do not fit. Members are expected to respect that call.

Hosts may also ask for certain members to **not attend** their events when the reasons are justifiable:

- It can apply to one event, or to all future events with that host.
- It is **not** used for minor things.
- Anyone on the list **will be told** before any event they cannot attend.

See the [Host Discretion List](/docs/event-staff-handbook/Hosts/host-discretion-list) for the process.
